Chrono Trigger

An innovative turn-based RPG where a dynamic action bar shapes every battle. Gather an unforgettable party, travel across eras, and change the course of history.

Release date
March 11, 1995
Platform

Genre

A timeless adventure

Across eras and emotions

The story

A journey through history

When an unexpected encounter opens a path through time, a young hero and a remarkable group of friends set out to protect the future. Each era reveals a new place, a new ally, and another piece of a story built around friendship and courage.

The battle system

Strategy in motion

Battles move with a dynamic action bar that decides when each character can act. Combine abilities, read the battlefield, and use powerful dual and triple techniques to turn every encounter into a moment of strategy.

The experience

A game that stays with you

Chrono Trigger balances wonder, humor, tension, and quiet tenderness with uncommon ease. Every second feels alive, making the adventure as emotionally memorable as it is exciting to play.
